How to pack

Get every shape inside the box, with nothing overlapping.

  • 1
    Drag shapes in and out of the box. Near an edge it snaps flush and straightens up.
  • 2
    Spin a shape by dragging its knob, scrolling, or pressing Q E. One degree at a time; hold Shift for 15 or twisting two fingers anywhere on the board, one degree at a time.
  • 3
    Nudge with the arrow keys, or type an exact angle at the bottom.Fine-tune with the ±1° buttons, or type an exact angle.
  • 4
    Dashed red means it overlaps or pokes out. A big smile means it fits.
