Fine print

Assumed

  • 2026 federal, 2025 California — the latest FTB has published.
  • State income tax estimated from your AGI, not your actual withholding.
  • Income flat in nominal terms; the assessment grows 2% a year.
  • Average balance is the year's open and close, per Pub. 936.

Not modelled

  • AMT, the 3.8% NIIT on rent, capital gains stacking.
  • Closing costs, points, transfer tax, and selling.
  • Opportunity cost of the down payment.
  • Suspended losses across years — the §469 allowance phases out well below these incomes anyway.
